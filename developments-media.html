Content that markets the build

Strum Media produces video, photography and aerial content for commercial property developments across Melbourne and Victoria. From office and retail to mixed-use and education projects, we capture the scale and progress of the build and turn it into content that markets it.

Whether you are driving pre-sales, showing progress to stakeholders or building your portfolio for the next project, we document the development from early works to completion — then edit it all in-house and deliver it ready for your channels and campaigns.

What we produce.

Videography

Progress, milestone and completion films that show the development taking shape.

Aerial & Drone

Aerial site coverage that captures the full scale, staging and location of the project.

Photography

Development and asset photography for marketing, sales, stakeholders and portfolio.

Content Creation

Social-first reels and campaign content built to drive pre-sales and leasing interest.
